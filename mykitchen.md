# My Kitchen

Reference for recipe suggestions. Keep this in mind whenever proposing meals or shopping lists.

## Equipment

- **Xiaomi rice cooker** — also steams and makes congee
- **Instant Pot** — pressure cook, slow cook, sauté
- **Amway Queen cookware** — pan, pot, larger pot (stainless, supports stack cooking / waterless cooking)
- **Oven**

## Household

- **Now (until 2026-06-03):** 2 adults + 1 baby (10.5 months)
- **From 2026-06-04:** 4 adults + 1 baby

Scale portions accordingly. Anything posted after June 4 should default to 4-adult quantities.

## Baby

- Age: 10.5 months
- **CMPA** (cow's milk protein allergy) — strict no dairy in any shared dish
- **No other allergies** — shellfish, fish, eggs, soy, wheat, peanuts/tree nuts (age-appropriate prep) all OK
  - No cow's milk, butter, cheese, yogurt, cream, ghee, whey, casein
  - Watch out for hidden dairy in bread, sauces, stocks, processed foods
  - Safe fats: olive oil, avocado oil, sesame oil, coconut oil
  - Calcium sources to lean on: tofu (calcium-set), leafy greens (bok choy, kale), sesame, sardines (bones), fortified oat/soy milk if introduced
- Eats what the adults eat — meals should be **cooked unseasoned**, then adults season their own plates at the table
- **Does not eat purées** — finger food / soft chewable pieces, cut to safe sizes (no whole grapes, no nuts, no honey under 12m)

## Cooking style

- **Healthy first** — minimal added oil, no MSG, low/no added salt during cooking, lean on whole ingredients
- **Cantonese-leaning**: steaming, stir-fry, soups (老火湯 / 滾湯), congee, blanched greens, claypot-style braises
- Occasional **pasta and noodles** (e.g. tomato sauces, simple olive oil + garlic, soy-based noodle bowls)
- Seasoning philosophy: salt, soy sauce, sesame oil, white pepper, ginger, scallion — added **at the table** for adults

## Proteins

- **No beef** at this time
- Yes to: chicken, pork (lean cuts), fish (white fish, salmon), shrimp, eggs, tofu, lentils/beans
- For baby: well-cooked, soft, no bones, no shells

## Quick recipe-suggestion guide

When suggesting a meal, default to:
1. One protein + one or two vegetables + one starch (rice / congee / noodles / potato)
2. Cooked in a way that works for the baby portion first, with adult seasoning at the end
3. Equipment-aware: pick the appliance that makes it easiest (e.g. congee in the Xiaomi cooker, soups in the Instant Pot, steamed fish on the Amway stack, roast veg in the oven)
4. CMPA-safe by construction — never propose dairy as part of the shared dish; suggest a dairy-free substitute if a "classic" recipe normally calls for it

- **Instant Pot steamed egg** (蒸水蛋) — silky savoury egg custard, baby loves it
- **Steamed fish with enoki underneath** on the Amway Queen — fish on a bed of enoki so it doesn't stick, steam-cooked

## Starter recipe set (10)

All recipes are written **baby-first**: cook unseasoned, plate baby's portion, then season adult plates with soy sauce / salt / sesame oil / white pepper at the table. Quantities are for **2 adults + baby**; double proteins and starches from June 4 onward.

### 1. Ginger chicken congee (薑雞粥)
- **Equipment:** Xiaomi rice cooker (congee mode)
- **Ingredients:** 1 cup jasmine rice, 1.5 boneless chicken thighs, 3 slices ginger, 8 cups water, 1 stalk scallion
- **Method:** Rinse rice, add to cooker with water and ginger. Lay chicken thighs on top. Run congee mode (~1.5 h). Lift chicken, shred finely, return to pot. Stir.
- **Baby:** serve as-is, shredded chicken + soft congee.
- **Adults:** white pepper, sesame oil, soy sauce, scallion on top.

### 2. Steamed pork patty with water chestnut (馬蹄蒸肉餅)
- **Equipment:** Amway Queen pot with steamer insert, or Instant Pot steam
- **Ingredients:** 250g minced pork (not too lean — ~20% fat for tenderness), 4 water chestnuts (finely diced), 1 tsp grated ginger, 1 tbsp cornstarch, 2 tbsp water, 1 egg white
- **Method:** Mix everything in one direction until sticky. Press flat into a heatproof plate. Steam 15 min over high heat until just set.
- **Baby:** scoop out a portion — soft, easy to gum. Break into small pieces.
- **Adults:** drizzle light soy + a few drops sesame oil at the table; top with scallion.

### 3. ABC soup (ABC湯)
- **Equipment:** Instant Pot (soup / pressure 25 min)
- **Ingredients:** 2 chicken drumsticks (or thighs, bone-in), 2 carrots, 2 potatoes, 1 onion, 1 corn cob (cut into rounds), 1 tomato, 8 cups water
- **Method:** Everything in. Pressure 25 min, natural release. Skim fat.
- **Baby:** soft carrot / potato / corn kernels off the cob, shredded chicken, a few sips of broth.
- **Adults:** salt + white pepper to taste at the table.

### 4. Garlic stir-fried bok choy (蒜蓉炒小棠菜)
- **Equipment:** Amway pan
- **Ingredients:** 1 bunch baby bok choy or choy sum, 3 garlic cloves (minced), 1 tsp olive or avocado oil, splash of water
- **Method:** Heat pan, add oil + garlic, 15 sec. Add greens, splash of water, cover 1 min. Toss until just wilted, stems still crisp.
- **Baby:** chop leaves finely, halve stems lengthwise so easy to chew.
- **Adults:** soy sauce / oyster sauce at the table (check oyster sauce label — most are dairy-free but verify).

### 5. Oven salmon parcel with zucchini and sweet potato
- **Equipment:** Oven, 200°C / 400°F
- **Ingredients:** 2 salmon fillets (skin off for baby's portion), 1 zucchini sliced, 1 small sweet potato cut into thin batons, ginger slices, 1 tsp sesame oil, parchment
- **Method:** Sweet potato batons need a head start — par-steam 5 min, or slice very thin. Build parcels: veg base, salmon on top, ginger slices, drizzle sesame oil. Seal parchment. Bake 15–18 min.
- **Baby:** flake salmon carefully (check for bones), soft zucchini, sweet potato batons as finger food.
- **Adults:** soy sauce + scallion at the table.

### 6. Tomato egg stir-fry (番茄炒蛋)
- **Equipment:** Amway pan
- **Ingredients:** 4 ripe tomatoes (cored, wedged), 4 eggs (beaten), 1 tsp olive oil, 1 tsp sugar (optional, to balance acidity — fine for baby in this small amount), splash of water
- **Method:** Soft-scramble eggs in oil, set aside just before fully set. In same pan, cook tomatoes with a splash of water until saucy (~5 min). Return eggs, fold through.
- **Baby:** serve over plain rice, eggs and tomato cut small.
- **Adults:** salt + white pepper at the table.

### 7. Instant Pot chicken & pumpkin stew
- **Equipment:** Instant Pot (pressure 12 min)
- **Ingredients:** 4 chicken thighs (boneless, skinless, bite-size), 400g kabocha or butternut (chunks), 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves, 1 thumb ginger sliced, 1 cup water or unsalted stock
- **Method:** Sauté onion + garlic + ginger 2 min. Add chicken, then pumpkin, then water. Pressure 12 min, quick release. Pumpkin will mostly break down into a sauce.
- **Baby:** soft pumpkin mashes on the fork, chicken shreds easily.
- **Adults:** salt + a splash of soy at the table; sesame oil + scallion finish.

### 8. Sesame-soy noodles with shredded chicken and greens
- **Equipment:** Amway pot (boil noodles) + pan (chicken & greens)
- **Ingredients:** 200g thin wheat noodles (check label — no milk/butter; udon or somen are good), 1 cooked chicken breast (poached or leftover), 1 bunch choy sum or spinach, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp light soy (for adult portion only)
- **Method:** Poach chicken in salted water 10 min, rest, shred. Boil noodles per packet. Blanch greens in the same water. Toss noodles with sesame oil, top with chicken + greens.
- **Baby:** cut noodles short with kitchen scissors, mix with chicken + chopped greens, tiny drizzle of sesame oil only.
- **Adults:** soy sauce + chili oil + scallion at the table.

### 9. Scrambled prawn with egg (滑蛋蝦仁)
- **Equipment:** Amway pan
- **Ingredients:** 200g raw peeled prawns (deveined, patted dry), 5 eggs beaten, 1 tsp cornstarch in 1 tbsp water (mixed into eggs), 1 tsp olive or avocado oil, 1 stalk scallion
- **Method:** Quickly sear prawns 1 min per side, just until pink, remove. Pour eggs into the pan over low heat, gently fold; when half set, return prawns and barely-set scallion. Pull off heat while still glossy.
- **Baby:** chop prawns very small (prawns can be a choking risk whole), serve over rice.
- **Adults:** white pepper + a few drops soy + sesame oil.

### 10. Oven-roasted chicken thighs with root vegetables
- **Equipment:** Oven, 200°C / 400°F
- **Ingredients:** 4 bone-in skin-on chicken thighs, 2 carrots, 2 parsnips or potatoes, 1 onion (wedges), 4 garlic cloves (skin on), 1 tbsp olive oil, fresh thyme or rosemary
- **Method:** Toss veg with oil + herbs on a tray. Lay chicken on top so juices drip down. Roast 35–40 min until skin is crisp and thighs hit 75°C internal.
- **Baby:** pull skinless meat off the bone, shred; vegetables are already soft. Skip the herbs on baby's portion if very strong.
- **Adults:** salt the chicken skin before roasting on the adult side of the tray; finish with flaky salt at the table.

## Expanded recipe set (11–20)

### 11. Steamed clams with ginger and scallion (薑蔥蜆)
- **Equipment:** Amway pan with lid
- **Ingredients:** 500g fresh clams (purged in salted water 1 h), 4 slices ginger, 2 scallions cut into batons, 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (optional, omit for baby's portion), 1 tsp sesame oil, splash of water
- **Method:** Heat pan, sauté ginger 30 sec. Add clams + 2 tbsp water, cover. Steam 3–5 min until shells just open. Toss in scallion + sesame oil off heat.
- **Baby:** pick clam meat out of shells, chop fine (clams without shell are choking-safe but small whole pieces can stick).
- **Adults:** light soy + a few drops sesame oil at the table.

### 12. Pork & lotus root soup (蓮藕排骨湯)
- **Equipment:** Instant Pot (pressure 35 min, natural release)
- **Ingredients:** 500g pork ribs (blanch first to remove scum), 1 medium lotus root (peeled, sliced thick), 6 dried red dates, 1 small handful peanuts (raw, for adult portion only — choking risk; can skip), 2 slices ginger, 8 cups water
- **Method:** Blanch ribs in boiling water 3 min, drain, rinse. Add everything to IP. Pressure 35 min, natural release.
- **Baby:** soft lotus root pieces (well-cooked, easy to gum), shredded pork off the bone, broth in a small cup.
- **Adults:** salt at the table.

### 13. Steamed silken tofu with minced pork topping
- **Equipment:** Amway steamer or Instant Pot steam
- **Ingredients:** 1 box silken tofu (~300g), 150g minced pork, 2 dried shiitake (soaked, diced fine), 1 tsp grated ginger, 1 tsp cornstarch, 1 tbsp water, 1 scallion
- **Method:** Cut tofu into thick slabs on a heatproof plate. Mix pork with shiitake, ginger, cornstarch, water. Spread pork mixture over tofu. Steam 12 min.
- **Baby:** tofu is naturally soft; cut pork topping small, mix with rice.
- **Adults:** soy sauce + sesame oil + scallion at the table.

### 14. Pumpkin congee with chicken (南瓜雞粥)
- **Equipment:** Xiaomi rice cooker (congee mode)
- **Ingredients:** 3/4 cup jasmine rice, 1/4 cup glutinous rice (for silkier texture, optional), 300g kabocha or butternut (cubed), 1 chicken thigh, 2 slices ginger, 8 cups water
- **Method:** Everything into the cooker. Congee mode. Pumpkin breaks down into the porridge. Shred chicken at the end.
- **Baby:** soft, naturally sweet from the pumpkin. No seasoning needed.
- **Adults:** white pepper + sesame oil + scallion + a dash of soy.

### 15. Steamed eggplant with garlic sauce (蒜蓉蒸茄子)
- **Equipment:** Amway pot with steamer insert
- **Ingredients:** 2 long Chinese eggplants, 4 garlic cloves (minced), 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp olive oil, 1 scallion, 1 tbsp light soy (adults only — finish at table)
- **Method:** Halve eggplants lengthwise (or steam whole and tear into strips after). Steam 10–12 min until very soft. Meanwhile, sauté garlic in oil until pale gold, off heat, add sesame oil. Spoon garlic oil over eggplant.
- **Baby:** very soft, easy to gum — serve plain or with a tiny drizzle of the garlic oil (no soy).
- **Adults:** soy sauce + scallion at the table.

### 16. Salmon fried rice
- **Equipment:** Amway pan
- **Ingredients:** 2 cups cold cooked rice (day-old is best), 1 leftover salmon fillet (flaked, bones checked), 2 eggs, 1/2 cup frozen peas + diced carrot, 2 scallions, 1 tbsp olive or avocado oil
- **Method:** Scramble eggs in oil, push to side. Add peas + carrot, stir 1 min. Add rice, break up, fry 3 min over high heat. Fold in salmon + scallions at the end.
- **Baby:** rice grains may be a choking step — make sure rice is moist, mix well, serve in small spoonfuls.
- **Adults:** soy sauce + white pepper + a drizzle of sesame oil at the table.

### 17. Aglio e olio with shrimp
- **Equipment:** Amway pot (pasta) + Amway pan (sauce)
- **Ingredients:** 200g spaghetti or linguine (check label — no egg/butter needed; standard durum semolina is dairy-free), 250g raw peeled shrimp (deveined), 5 garlic cloves (thinly sliced), 3 tbsp olive oil, fresh parsley, lemon
- **Method:** Boil pasta in salted water, reserve 1/2 cup pasta water. In the pan, gently warm olive oil with garlic until pale gold (don't burn). Add shrimp, cook 2 min. Add pasta + a splash of pasta water, toss. Finish with parsley + squeeze of lemon.
- **Baby:** chop shrimp into small pieces, cut pasta short with kitchen scissors. Skip chili flakes on baby's side.
- **Adults:** chili flakes + black pepper + extra olive oil at the table.

### 18. Sheet-pan shrimp with broccoli and bell pepper
- **Equipment:** Oven, 220°C / 425°F
- **Ingredients:** 400g large raw peeled shrimp, 1 head broccoli (florets), 1 red bell pepper (strips), 3 garlic cloves (minced), 2 tbsp olive oil, lemon
- **Method:** Toss broccoli + pepper + 1 tbsp oil on the tray. Roast 10 min. Add shrimp tossed with remaining oil + garlic. Roast another 6–8 min until shrimp are pink and just opaque.
- **Baby:** chop shrimp small (whole shrimp = choking risk), broccoli florets cut to safe pieces.
- **Adults:** flaky salt + lemon + chili flakes at the table.

### 19. One-pot chicken & shiitake rice (冬菇滑雞飯)
- **Equipment:** Xiaomi rice cooker (standard rice mode)
- **Ingredients:** 1.5 cups jasmine rice (rinsed) + water per cooker line, 2 boneless chicken thighs (bite-size), 6 dried shiitake (soaked, sliced; use the soaking water as part of the rice liquid for extra umami), 1 thumb ginger (julienned), 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp olive oil, 1 scallion
- **Method:** Toss chicken with sesame oil, olive oil, ginger, shiitake. Add rice + soaking water + plain water to cooker line. Lay chicken + shiitake on top. Standard rice cycle. Rest 10 min, fluff, scatter scallion.
- **Baby:** chicken should be very tender — shred a portion; chop shiitake fine.
- **Adults:** a small drizzle of light soy + sesame oil at the table.

### 20. Miso-glazed baked cod with bok choy
- **Equipment:** Oven, 200°C / 400°F
- **Ingredients:** 2–3 cod or black cod fillets, 1 tbsp white miso, 1 tsp mirin (or 1/2 tsp sugar + splash water), 1 tsp sesame oil, ginger slices, 2 heads baby bok choy
- **Method:** Reserve **one fillet plain** for baby (just ginger + a drop of sesame oil). Whisk miso + mirin + sesame oil; brush over the adult fillets, marinate 15 min. Place all fish + halved bok choy on a parchment-lined tray. Bake 12–14 min until fish flakes.
- **Baby:** plain cod (no miso — sodium too high for under 1), bok choy chopped fine.
- **Adults:** the miso glaze does the seasoning; finish with scallion.

## Freezer-friendly baby portions

Cook once, eat for weeks. Use **silicone ice cube trays** (~30 ml cubes) for single servings, pop out frozen and store in labelled freezer bags. Thaw overnight in the fridge or reheat from frozen in the steamer / microwave with a splash of water. **Use within 1 month** for best texture and nutrition.

### Freezes beautifully
- **Congee (#1, #14):** freeze in cubes; add a splash of water when reheating to loosen. The pumpkin congee (#14) is especially good — naturally sweet, no seasoning needed.
- **ABC soup (#3):** strain solids, freeze veg + chicken pieces in one bag, broth in another (or together in cubes). Reheats to fresh.
- **Chicken & pumpkin stew (#7):** the pumpkin sauce is freezer-perfect. Mash slightly before freezing for easy spoon-feeding texture.
- **Lotus root pork soup (#12):** broth + shredded pork freeze well; lotus root softens further on reheat (fine for baby).
- **Pork patty (#2):** steam, cool, slice and freeze in small pieces. Re-steam 5 min to refresh.
- **Steamed tofu with pork topping (#13):** **freeze the pork topping only** — silken tofu turns spongy after freezing. Steam fresh tofu when serving and reheat the topping over it.

### Freezes OK with a tweak
- **Steamed fish, salmon (#5, #20):** flake the cooked fish, mix into a spoonful of broth or soup before freezing — prevents drying out. Reheat gently.
- **Salmon fried rice (#16):** freezes acceptably; reheat with a splash of water under cover to revive. Best fresh, but a backup is fine.
- **Roast chicken (#10, #19):** shred meat off the bone, mix with a little of its pan juices or unsalted stock, freeze in cubes.

### Don't freeze
- Stir-fried greens (#4, #15 eggplant is borderline — gets watery)
- Anything egg-based that's not custard (scrambles go rubbery): #6, #9, IP steamed egg
- Noodles and pasta (#8, #17) — cook fresh
- Raw shellfish dishes (#11 clams, #18 shrimp) — cook fresh

### Weekly batch-cook suggestion
On Sunday, make one big pot of congee (#1 or #14) **and** one stew/soup (#7 or #12). Portion both into ~10 cubes each. That's ~20 baby meals on standby — backup for nursery days, sick days, or when the adult dinner isn't baby-appropriate.
